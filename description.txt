Developed **OpTwin**, a browser-based pharmaceutical manufacturing digital twin prototype integrating **OR, ML, RL (PPO) and XAI** to simulate and optimize a virtual nine-zone OSD manufacturing facility. Implemented **event-driven simulation, constraint-aware heuristic scheduling, Monte Carlo analysis, weighted-graph routing and PPO-based adaptive material dispatch** to improve simulated throughput, resource utilization and efficiency. Built **supervised ML models for equipment failure prediction and Remaining Useful Life (RUL) estimation**, with asset-grouped validation, classification/regression benchmarking and custom Shapley-based feature attribution. Integrated **stochastic scenario and sensitivity analysis, engineering economics,** factory visualization, performance analytics and data import/export.
